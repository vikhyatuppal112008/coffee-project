Seed to Cup Philosophy

Rooted in Indian Estates & Living Greenhouses

Founded to celebrate shade-grown Indian specialty lots alongside culinary botanicals, Beans & Bloom roasts in small batches and pairs every harvest with organic florals, spices, and wild forest honey.

01 · Direct Estates

Chikmagalur & Araku

We partner directly with family estates at 4,200+ ft elevation, paying 35% above commodity farm-gate benchmarks for washed and honey-processed lots.

02 · Botanical Infusions

House-Steeped Syrups

Zero artificial essences. Every syrup is cold-macerated in-house using Kannauj damask rose, Malabar green cardamom, and Nilgiri chamomile.

03 · Zero-Waste Loop

Composted Chaff & Grounds

Spent espresso pucks and roasting chaff nourish our conservatory ferns and local urban herb growers across the neighborhood.
