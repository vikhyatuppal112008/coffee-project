/ Our Story
Rooted in Indian Terroir

Cultivating Slow Mornings Through Coffee & Flora.

Founded in 2019, Beans & Bloom began with a desire to honor India’s historic coffee-growing highlands while creating an urban botanical refuge. We work directly with shade-grown estates in Baba Budangiri, Chikmagalur and Araku Valley, Andhra Pradesh—roasting small batches on our Loring smart roaster to highlight natural floral, stone-fruit, and cocoa sweetness.

Inside our glasshouse conservatory, our botanical apothecary distills and cold-steeps organic Pampore saffron, Kannauj damask rosewater, wild Himalayan honey, and garden rosemary to craft syrups that elevate rather than mask specialty espresso.
