Our Philosophy

Cultivating Connection Through Coffee & Flora.

Beans & Bloom began with a simple dream: to unite specialty third-wave coffee roasting with the restorative tranquility of a botanical greenhouse. We partner directly with high-altitude family farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, roasting in small batches every Tuesday and Friday.

Ethical Direct-Trade Sourcing

We pay 35% above Fair Trade minimums to support regenerative agroforestry and farm-level sustainability.

Fresh Botanical Infusions

Our syrups are cold-steeped in-house using organic culinary lavender, rosemary, cardamom pods, and wildflower honey.

Cozy Community Conservatory

Sun-drenched reading nooks, acoustic Sunday mornings, and a curated plant shop where every table centerpiece is adoptable.
